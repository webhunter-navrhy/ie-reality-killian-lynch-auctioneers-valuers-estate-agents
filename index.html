***4 BEDROOM BUNGALOW AND GARAGE ON C.0.64 ACRES***

Killian Lynch Auctioneers are pleased to present this spacious 4 bedroom bungalow and garage set on a private site of C.0.64 acres. The property which comprises of C.1,700 sq ft living accommodation boasts a bright open plan kitchen/dining room, generous living room and also has a converted attic. This attractive family home offers countryside living while remaining within each reach of local amenities. Located only C.5km from Terelton village, C.10 minutes drive to Macroom town and C.40 minutes drive to Cork city. Viewings highly advised.
